# Z plant with the friction measured on the bench 22 Oct (warm, 0.2-10 rad/s
# plus id_sys 5-50 rad/s): Stribeck rise below 1 rad/s, more friction moving
# up, gravity 4.48 Nm, breakaway 2.4 Nm up / 1.6 Nm down from rest (id_brk).
# Up, down, up 20 mm at 73 mm/s and 1000 mm/s2, 0.9 s apart; the current is
# read at the end of each dwell (after up 0.85-1.0 s, after down 1.75-1.9 s).
# pid0.hold_en 2 flags rest and freezes the integrator, dob0 slews its
# compensation so the torque goes to conf0.o: 17.6 / 3.3 A -> 10.8 / 10.8 A.
# The hold starts inside pid0.hold_arm (0.1 mrad), so the rest error is about
# that, not the 2 mrad hold_win (the bench parked at 1.99 mrad before hold_arm).
# dob0.max 8: 4 stops the slew short.
title Z standstill hold in dob (hold_en 2, dob0.hold), dob as on the bench, max 8
config conf/fanuc_a6-3000.txt
plant f=2.195 fst=0.894 vs=0.539 fh=0.701 fh_n=0.285 vh=4.66 d=0.0089 fs=0.2 fs_n=-0.6
f4 link filters
f4 dob0.j = 0.00595
f4 dob0.bw = 30
f4 dob0.max = 8
f4 dob0.k = 1
f4 conf0.f = 0
f4 conf0.d = 0
lcnc scale 10
at 0.05 lcnc enable 1
at 0.06 plant load=-4.48
f4 pid0.hold_en = 2
f4 dob0.hold = pid0.hold
f4 pid0.hold_o = 4.59
f4 dob0.hold_o = 4.59
at 0.1 lcnc move 20 73 1000
at 1.0 lcnc move -20 73 1000
at 1.9 lcnc move 20 73 1000
trace lcnc.pos plant.pos_mm plant.vel pid0.pos_error pid0.torque_cmd pid0.torque_sum pid0.ff_torque_cmd pid0.hold hv0.iq_fb dob0.d_est dob0.out
expect max fault0.last_fault < 0.5 from 0.06
expect mean hv0.iq_fb > 9.5 from 0.85 to 1.0
expect mean hv0.iq_fb < 12 from 0.85 to 1.0
expect mean hv0.iq_fb > 9.5 from 1.75 to 1.9
expect mean hv0.iq_fb < 12 from 1.75 to 1.9
expect absmax pid0.pos_error < 0.0002 from 0.85 to 1.0
expect absmax pid0.pos_error < 0.0002 from 1.75 to 1.9
run 2.8
